## Hi there 👋

I recently graduated from a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Compouter Science with a specialised focus on AI and ML, at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science (FRI) in Ljubljana.

I have enrolled in the MPhil in Advanced Computer Science program at Cambridge University, which I will start this October! I am very excited for the opportunity to study at Cambridge and look forward to further pursuing my research interests.

I work as a researcher at FRI's Machine Learning Lab - Previously, I worked on building a new opne source state-of-the-art English to Slovene translation system, which I have published a paper about at an ECAI Workshop ([see the paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.luhme-1.7/)) and co-authored the [paper on developing GaMS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01691) (a Slovene open-weights LLM).

More recently, I have been working on a project where I build on classical LLMs and enable them to understand **graphs**, unifying the knowledge of pretrained LLMs in textual domains and the power of Graph Transformers. A preprint on the architecture and my findings is [available on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10247). I am open to discussions and potential collaborations in this field.
